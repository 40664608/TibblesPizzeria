Help Page
How to earn XP? To earn XP, focus on making high-quality pizzas and ensuring customer orders are accurate. Completing tasks on time without errors will maximize your rewards. Providing fast service is essential, as keeping customers waiting can reduce your score. At the end of each day, your overall performance rating will also contribute to the amount of XP you receive.
